PUMPKIN MANOR - FREE SAMPLE - Halloween Floor Tiles [32x32]
Version 1.0 - by Chucky Assets

This free sample is sheet A of Pumpkin Manor: 48 floor tiles and floor overlays.
The full pack (205 sprites: autotiling walls, furniture, pumpkins, ghosts,
story props and more) is here: https://chuckyassets.itch.io/pumpkin-manor

INCLUDED
sheets/A_floors.png + A_floors.json   48 sprites on a 32x32 grid
sprites/A_floors/                     every sprite as its own PNG
palette/                              the 40-colour palette
examples/                             a floor layout made from these tiles

- Variants of one material (wood, stone, checker, carpet) tile seamlessly
  with each other, so mix them freely.
- Rugs are 9-slice (tl t tr / l c r / bl b br): any rug 2x2 tiles or larger.
- The magic circle is 2x2 tiles; cobweb corners go in the matching corner.
- Overlays (ovl_*) have transparency: put them on a layer above the floor.
- Scale by whole numbers with nearest-neighbour filtering; no anti-aliasing.
